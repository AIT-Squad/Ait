## Engineering responsibility

This project follows the
[10x Responsible Engineering Covenant](RESPONSIBILITY-COVENANT.md).

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Contributors remain responsible
for understanding, reviewing, testing, securing, and documenting
everything they submit.

Our current maintenance and support status is published in
[`STEWARDSHIP.yaml`](STEWARDSHIP.yaml).

![10xREC: Active Stewardship](badges/active.svg)

> You may use AI to produce code.  
> You may not use AI to outsource responsibility.
>
> **10x the speed. 10x the responsibility.**
