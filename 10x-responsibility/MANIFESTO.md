# The 10x Responsibility Manifesto

AI can help me write code I do not fully understand.

I choose not to make that someone else's problem.

I will not confuse generation with engineering.

I will not confuse passing tests with understanding.

I will not confuse more code with more value.

I will not present experimental code as production-ready.

I will not hide uncertainty behind confident documentation,
clean abstractions, or machine-generated explanations.

If I submit it, I review it.

If I publish it, I describe its limits.

If I maintain it, I communicate its status.

If I leave it, I leave honestly.

I may use AI to produce code.

I will not use AI to outsource responsibility.

**10x the speed. 10x the responsibility.**
